Named protocol for GrokOS-zt4p. Run =just review-clock=.

* Arms

- =fsrs=: =recall()= due-first; =schedule_review(recalled=True)= on a hit
- =always-include=: trust-only live dump; =due_at= ignored
- =never-review=: clear =due_at= after the shared first test

Fixture: 8 lessons (trust 0.01) + 72 distractors (trust 9.0),
limit=8, day0 =2026-08-17T00:00:00.000Z=, seats on days 1-7.

* Measured

keep-test 0.429/0.000/0.000 (FSRS/always/never); week-later R
0.949/0.484/0.484

keep-test is lesson appearances in day-1..7 splices over 56
opportunities. Week-later R is mean FSRS-lite retrievability of
the lesson set at day 7. Packset SHA =1cb90b92e74c2c59a139707ece4bfd7168586e80=.

Numbers come from =scripts/review_clock_eval.py=. They are not
invented. JSON: [[file:../../research/review-clock-three-cell.json][research/review-clock-three-cell.json]].
